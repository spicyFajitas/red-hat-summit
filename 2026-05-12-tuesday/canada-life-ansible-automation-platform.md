From community to enterprise: Canada Life and Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform

As enterprises scale their automation efforts, the limitations of community-supported tools become increasingly apparent. At Canada Life, we faced these challenges head-on—navigating compliance requirements, operational overhead, and the need for a more strategic automation approach. In this session, attendees will gain practical insights into how a managed automation platform can unlock engineering capacity, improve compliance posture, and drive enterprise-wide automation maturity. We will: 

Discuss the enterprise challenges of running community tools, including compliance, scalability, and support limitations.
Share how migrating to Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform empowered our engineering teams to shift from platform maintenance to delivering automation as a product.
Explore how adopting a product delivery model transformed our platform team’s role and accelerated innovation.
Demonstrate our integration of Splunk and Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform 2.6 to enable Event-Driven Ansible, supporting our AIOps initiatives and proactive incident response.
